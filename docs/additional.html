The venue

Note
  • Note our new venue! We will be hosting in-person participants at 275 George Street, Brisbane.

The venue (275 George Street, Brisbane) is the location of Fisheries Queensland’s head office. It is a short, 550m walk from Central train station, or 600m to Roma Street train station.

All public transport fares in Queensland cost 50 cents. Contactless payment is available on all South East Queensland (SEQ) trains, trams, ferries and buses—you can use your digital or physical bank card to tap on and off.

The workshop will be held in a boardroom on Level 14. Participants are to meet on the ground floor and a designated Queensland Government staff member will escort you in.

Wi-Fi will be available on site.

Census

Tuesday night is Census night! If you’re staying away from home on Tuesday night to attend our workshop, read up on how you should complete the census. Note the following instructions taken from the ABS website:

Staying at a hotel, motel, or other holiday accommodation (including Bed and Breakfasts)

  • If you’re staying in a hotel, motel or other holiday accommodation away from home, you’ll need to complete a Census form.
  • The accommodation staff will hand out Census forms to all guests and collect completed forms. Please check with staff if you don’t get a form.
  • If you prefer, you can complete the Census online wherever you can access the internet. Use the login details on the front of your paper form or you can get a Census number. You’ll need to use a mobile phone to complete the process of getting a Census number and password.

Staying in short-term accommodation (Airbnb, Stayz or a holiday rental)

  • You’ll need to complete a Census form wherever you are staying on Census night.
  • If you’re staying at short-term accommodation such as an Airbnb or Stayz, the easiest way to complete your Census is online.
  • You can get a Census number and complete your Census online wherever you have access to the internet. You’ll need to use a mobile phone to complete the process of getting a Census number and password.
