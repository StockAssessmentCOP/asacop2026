Poster session

As a jurisdiction, bring a digital poster representing your work, team, or context. This is intentionally flexible! Ideas include:

  • a flagship stock assessment
  • a challenging case study
  • your team and region (“getting to know us”)
  • a methodological innovation
  • a collaboration success story
